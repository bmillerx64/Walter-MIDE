GS339 adds a presentation-only `SETUP BUILDING · WATCH CLOSELY` cue for consecutive-scan transitions that are:

- above VWAP and within 2.5%;
- SuperTrend bullish;
- participation >= 28 and expansion >= 45;
- either a fresh VWAP reclaim or materially strengthening participation/expansion;
